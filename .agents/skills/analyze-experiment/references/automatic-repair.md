# Automatic repair evidence


Fast path and recorded-actions now default on, trust threshold is 1, and a broker-validated program revision preserves prior success credit. A real fast-path failure still resets trust. Selection attempts one trusted program/binding by distinct covered findings, then stable program ID/bindings order. Uncovered findings, ambiguous alternatives and unexplained drift no longer cause pre-apply refusal. Fast-path application verification is per cause: every selected bound diagnosis must return NoMatch, its covered finding fingerprints must be absent from fresh successful detector evaluations, and no new finding fingerprint may appear on its named bindings, write targets or covered resources (including findings that name those resources as related or parameter bindings). Other findings from the same detector may remain active. A verified repair is kept and the responder is dispatched immediately for the remaining findings with typed `IncidentRequest.fast_path_repairs` (program ID/revision, bindings, covered findings, verification time). Whole-incident verification still decides closure. Only a still-matching bound diagnosis or a newly introduced finding triggers fast-path rollback and program trust failure; unavailable or contradictory verification hands off without a trust penalty or verified credit. Historical skip-reason strings remain readable but removed coverage/revision/policy gates are no longer emitted.

Plan patch Authorization now reports value provenance (`accepted-source`, `parameter`, `observed`, `incident-namespace`) rather than a source/reviewed-fixture grant. Historical `reviewed-fixture` and `incident-named-delete` receipts retain their original meaning. Value-provenance reporting preserves the existing outcome and result layouts; controller-config policy tags 72–74 are retired and reserved. No policy-loaded startup event or `SDO_PLAN_SOURCE_POLICY_FILE` is expected. Defaults: 10 loops, 5 validation retries, 5-minute verification, menu on unless explicitly disabled.

Current fast-path receipts may contain completed Go settlement with version-1 reflection eligibility, rather than the retired `trusted program fast path` skip string. The whole-incident `fast_path` label requires matching programs, applied verified plans, no rollback or failure signal, no responder Job, acknowledged commits and complete accepted reflection. A kept partial repair instead leaves `resolution_path=responder`; count its program as a fast-path success from the application’s cause-scoped proof, even though a responder Job handles the remaining causes.

`PlanApplication.covered_findings_verified` is optional: present true records cause-scoped success; present false records an inconclusive or failed cause-scoped attempt; absent preserves legacy whole-incident semantics. Cause-scoped trust success requires retained exact plan fields and bound post-apply NoMatch evidence, and does not depend on aggregate incident classification. A failed peer cause cannot reset that program’s trust. Newly appearing unrelated peer findings also remain neutral and are handed to the responder. Marker states are `attempting`, `resolved`, `partial`, `unverified`, `skipped`, and `failed`; only `failed` restores recorded trust failure signals. The marker captures pre-apply baseline/covered findings and bound diagnoses. Restart independently rechecks them without replaying writes, rollback handles or cached success.
