func (w Workload) SamplingPlan() SamplingPlan
    SamplingPlan builds the workload's plan. A health probe samples every
    scenario at least once per sample interval (MinSampleInterval unless the
    workload sets SampleInterval) regardless of weight: each cycle starts
    with one iteration of every scenario, and the iterations the workload's
    rate leaves over in that interval are shared by weight. When the rate
    cannot cover every scenario once per interval it is raised to do so.
    Other workloads (bursts, journeys) follow their weights only.

func (w Workload) ScenarioArrival(id string) time.Duration
    ScenarioArrival is the expected time between samples of one scenario under
    the workload's SamplingPlan. Zero when the rate or the scenario is unknown.

func (w Workload) ScenarioHorizon(id string) time.Duration
    ScenarioHorizon is how long the scenario's evaluation window needs to hold
    only samples taken after a given moment.

func (w Workload) ScenarioSLO(id string) SLO
    ScenarioSLO is the effective SLO of one scenario of the workload.

func (w Workload) Validate() error
    Validate reports the first problem that makes the workload unsafe.

func (w Workload) ValidateAgainst(catalog Catalog) error
    ValidateAgainst checks that every scenario the workload names exists in the
    catalog. A health probe may hold only write scenarios.

func (w Workload) WarmupBound() time.Duration
    WarmupBound is how long a health probe may warm up after it starts or
    resets: the time its steady rate needs to give every scenario its SLO's
    minimum samples, plus one sample interval for the iteration in flight.
    A warm-up never lasts longer than steady sampling would have taken,
    so a scenario that cannot record usable samples (its probe user is not
    provisioned) does not hold the probe at the warm-up rate.

func (w Workload) WarmupRate() float64
    WarmupRate is the iterations per second a health probe runs at while it
    warms up (Engine.UseWarmup): its sampling plan's cycle compressed into
    MinSampleInterval, never below its steady rate. A started or reset probe
    holds no samples, and its detectors stay unconfirmed until every scenario
    holds its SLO's MinSamples; at the steady rate that takes MinSamples sample
    intervals (a designer lengthens the interval up to MaxSampleInterval to
    lower the steady load), at this rate MinSamples floor intervals. The extra
    load lasts those few intervals, the same plan at the floor interval every
    health probe may run at. Other workloads keep their rate.

func (w Workload) WithDefaults() Workload
    WithDefaults fills unset fields.
